# Fashion Commerce

The shared language for a modern fashion commerce platform that sells apparel and accessories directly to customers.

## Language

**Store**:
The retail brand and storefront through which customers browse products and place orders.
_Avoid_: shop, storefront, brand

**Customer**:
A person or organization purchasing products from the store.
_Avoid_: user, buyer, account

**Product**:
A sellable item in the catalog, such as a dress, jacket, or accessory.
_Avoid_: item, SKU

**Variant**:
A specific combination of product attributes such as size, color, or fit that can carry its own stock and price.
_Avoid_: option

**Collection**:
A curated group of products tied to a seasonal or editorial story.
_Avoid_: category

**Cart**:
The in-progress set of product variants a customer intends to buy.
_Avoid_: basket

**Order**:
A customer’s accepted purchase request, including items, shipping, payment, and fulfillment commitments.
_Avoid_: purchase, transaction

**Promotion**:
A pricing or eligibility rule that changes the order value, such as discounts, bundles, thresholds, or gift cards.
_Avoid_: deal

**Fulfillment**:
The operational process of preparing, shipping, and delivering an order.
_Avoid_: logistics

**Return**:
A customer-requested reversal or exchange of an order or line item.
_Avoid_: refund, cancellation

**Locale**:
A market-specific language, currency, and regional policy set.
_Avoid_: language, region

**Currency**:
The monetary unit used to price and settle a purchase in a locale.
_Avoid_: money

**Admin**:
The internal operational interface for managing product content, inventory, promotions, orders, and returns.
_Avoid_: backend
